# Dialog 02: Noisy neighbours

**Level:** B1-B2 · **Setting:** two friends over coffee, Dan complains and Lena advises  
**Length:** 323 words, about 2.5 min spoken · **Idioms:** 10

## Dialogue

**Lena:** You look tired, Dan. Did something happen at home? You've been yawning since you arrived.  
**Dan:** My neighbours happened. They play loud music until two in the morning, and I really can't **put up with** it any longer.  
**Lena:** Have you talked to them? Sometimes people just don't realise how loud they are.  
**Dan:** I tried once, but I started to **beat around the bush**. I said something like "it's a bit late for music, isn't it?"  
**Lena:** That's the problem. Be direct. Just say it clearly, and don't **beat around the bush**.  
**Dan:** Easy for you to say. The guy upstairs **gets on my nerves** every time I see him. I'm afraid I'll lose my temper.  
**Lena:** Then you need to **hold back** your emotions at first. Speak calmly, and he'll listen more.  
**Dan:** I'll try. Last week I **passed by** his door and wanted to knock, but I walked away.  
**Lena:** Maybe next time you won't walk away. Do you know anything about them? Are they students?  
**Dan:** No idea. They **keep to themselves**. They never say hello, they just nod and disappear into their flat.  
**Lena:** Maybe they are shy. Or maybe they work nights and don't think about anyone else. Be fair to them.  
**Dan:** You're right. I wanted to write them a note, but it **slipped my mind** twice already.  
**Lena:** Write it now, right here, on your phone. Mention the time and ask them to turn the music down after eleven.  
**Dan:** Okay. And if they don't **take** it **seriously**? I'll have to call the landlord.  
**Lena:** Then you do that, but give them a chance first. Most people react well to a polite message.  
**Dan:** I hope so. I just want to **get over** this problem and finally sleep a full night.  
**Lena:** You will. Send the note tonight and tell me how it goes tomorrow.  
**Dan:** Deal. Thanks, Lena. You always know what to say when I'm stressed.  
**Lena:** That's what friends are for. Now finish your coffee, you look like a zombie!
