# Dialog 11: Health & Fitness Goals

**Level:** B1  
**Duration:** 3-4 min  
**Key idioms:** 4

## Characters
- **Trainer:** Alex
- **Client:** Sophie

## Full Dialogue

**Alex:** Sophie! How are your fitness goals going?

**Sophie:** Honestly, I'm wiped out. Training around the clock is harder than I thought.

**Alex:** That's normal! It seems to me you're making great progress though.

**Sophie:** Really? I want to push harder and not fall short on my potential.

**Alex:** I like that attitude! Let's hang out and work through this together.

**Sophie:** Thanks! I'm all set to commit to this fully!

---

**Q: How do you describe physical exhaustion?**  
**Hint (RU):** Полностью уставший, измученный
