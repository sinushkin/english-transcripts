# Dialog 04: Planning a trip abroad

**Level:** B1-B2 · **Setting:** Nina and Alex plan a year of travelling  
**Length:** 339 words, about 2.6 min spoken · **Idioms:** 13

## Dialogue

**Nina:** Alex, I have big news. I'm going to **take a year off** work and travel through South America!  
**Alex:** Seriously? That's a huge decision. Are you sure you're ready to **take a risk** like that?  
**Nina:** Absolutely. I've been a **rolling stone** my whole life. I can't sit in one place for more than a year.  
**Alex:** True, I remember. You never stay long anywhere. When do you plan to **set off**?  
**Nina:** In March. I want to leave at the **crack of dawn** on the first day, so I can reach the airport before the crowds.  
**Alex:** Then I'll come and **see** you **off**. I'll bring coffee and a sandwich for the road.  
**Nina:** That's so sweet. I also need to **put aside** some money, because the trip is expensive.  
**Alex:** How much have you saved? You said you would **put aside** a third of your salary each month.  
**Nina:** Almost enough. I think I can **get by** on thirty dollars a day if I stay in cheap hostels.  
**Alex:** That sounds tight, but possible. How will you **get around** between the cities? By bus?  
**Nina:** Mostly buses and sometimes trains. Locals use public transport everywhere, so it's easy to **get around**.  
**Alex:** Do you want me to **put together** a list of useful apps for you? I travelled a lot last year.  
**Nina:** Yes, please! And one more thing, I've been **putting off** buying travel insurance. It's boring, but I know it's necessary.  
**Alex:** Don't put it off any longer! Do it this week, because accidents happen when you least expect them.  
**Nina:** You're right. I'll buy it tomorrow. Then I'll probably **lose track of time** planning the route in the evening.  
**Alex:** Ha, you always do. Maybe I should **put forward** a different idea: why don't I join you for the first month?  
**Nina:** Really? That would be wonderful! I'd feel much safer with a friend, at least for the beginning.  
**Alex:** Let me talk to my boss. If she says yes, we'll **set off** together in March.  
**Nina:** I'm so happy. This trip is going to change my life, I can feel it.
