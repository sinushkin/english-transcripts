# Dialog 4: Restaurant Ordering

**Level:** A2  
**Duration:** 3-4 min  
**Key idioms:** 3

## Characters
- **Server:** Michael
- **Customer:** Lisa

## Full Dialogue

**Michael:** Welcome! What can I get you?

**Lisa:** I'm starving! I've been working around the clock all week. What do you recommend?

**Michael:** Our signature pasta is great. Most customers eat it all up in minutes!

**Lisa:** Perfect! I'll take that. And actually, I'm all set with just water, thanks.

**Michael:** Great choice. Can I take your coat?

**Lisa:** Yes, thanks! This restaurant seems to be the talk of the town.

**Michael:** It really is! Your food will be out soon. Enjoy!
