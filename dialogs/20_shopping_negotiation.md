# Dialog 20: Shopping & Negotiation

**Level:** B1  
**Duration:** 3-4 min  
**Key idioms:** 4

## Characters
- **Seller:** Robert
- **Buyer:** Sandra

## Full Dialogue

**Sandra:** This jacket is beautiful, but the price is high. I've been shopping around the clock for a good deal.

**Robert:** It seems to me this is our best quality. I understand your concern.

**Sandra:** Can you offer a discount? I don't want to fall short on quality, but I also need good value.

**Robert:** Let me talk to my manager. We can hang out for a few minutes?

**Sandra:** Sure! If you give me a 15% discount, I'm all set to buy it!
