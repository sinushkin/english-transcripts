# Dialog 08: At the doctor's

**Level:** B2 · **Setting:** a patient with dizzy spells talks to Dr. Evans  
**Length:** 301 words, about 2.3 min spoken · **Idioms:** 11

## Dialogue

**Dr. Evans:** Good morning, Mr. Brown. What brings you here today? You look a bit pale.  
**Patient:** Good morning, doctor. For the last few weeks I've been having **dizzy spells**, mostly in the morning.  
**Dr. Evans:** I see. Did you **put off** coming to the doctor? These things shouldn't be ignored.  
**Patient:** Yes, I did. I kept telling myself it would pass, and I didn't want to **make** a fuss.  
**Dr. Evans:** I understand, but you must **take** this **seriously**. Dizziness can have many causes, and some are important.  
**Patient:** My wife says I work too much. I'm a bit of a **workaholic**, and I often skip lunch.  
**Dr. Evans:** That could be one reason. How many hours do you work, **outside of work** commitments included?  
**Patient:** About twelve hours a day. Sometimes more, because I answer emails at night.  
**Dr. Evans:** That's far too much. Frankly, I won't **beat around the bush**: your lifestyle is probably the problem.  
**Patient:** I was afraid you'd say that. I'm not young any more. As we **get older**, it's harder to ignore our body.  
**Dr. Evans:** Exactly. But it's not too late. First, I'll check your blood pressure, and we'll do some tests.  
**Patient:** Of course. How long will I wait for the results?  
**Dr. Evans:** About three days. My nurse will **get back to** you by phone as soon as they arrive.  
**Patient:** Thank you. Is there anything I can do to **get by** until then? I still have to work.  
**Dr. Evans:** Drink plenty of water, eat regular meals, and **take** a short **break** every two hours.  
**Patient:** I'll try, though it won't be easy to **get through the day** without coffee.  
**Dr. Evans:** Cut it down gradually. And please, **take your time** to recover. Your health matters more than any deadline.  
**Patient:** You're right. I'll talk to my boss today and ask for a few days off.  
**Dr. Evans:** Good decision. Come back next week, and we'll discuss the results.
