# Dialog 14: Student-Teacher Discussion

**Level:** B2  
**Duration:** 3-4 min  
**Key idioms:** 4

## Characters
- **Student:** Oliver
- **Professor:** Dr. Wilson

## Full Dialogue

**Oliver:** Professor, I want to discuss my essay. I feel like I fell short on the analysis.

**Dr. Wilson:** Oliver, it seems to me you put good effort in. What specifically concerns you?

**Oliver:** The structure. I've been thinking about it around the clock, but I'm still not satisfied.

**Dr. Wilson:** Let's work together. I'm all set to help you improve. Don't be afraid to take on revisions!

**Oliver:** Thank you! I'll break a leg with the next draft!

---
