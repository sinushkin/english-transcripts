# Dialog 12: Running at dawn

**Level:** B1 · **Setting:** two friends, Mia and Leo, start a morning running routine  
**Length:** 282 words, about 2.2 min spoken · **Idioms:** 12

## Dialogue

**Leo:** Mia, why are you texting me at five in the morning? It's still dark outside!  
**Mia:** Because we agreed to **take up** running, remember? I'm ready, and I'm standing at your door.  
**Leo:** Seriously? It's the **crack of dawn**! Nobody normal does sports at this hour.  
**Mia:** The park is empty now, and the air is fresh. Come on, stop making excuses and **get over** your laziness.  
**Leo:** Okay, okay. I always **put off** things like this, and I know it's bad. Give me five minutes.  
**Mia:** I'll wait. By the way, don't **hold back** at the start. Run at your own pace, and don't worry about me.  
**Leo:** Good, because I'll be slow. Last week I had **dizzy spells** after the stairs. I'm in terrible shape.  
**Mia:** Then we must **take** it **seriously**. If you feel dizzy, stop immediately and tell me.  
**Leo:** Deal. Let's **set off**. Which route are we taking?  
**Mia:** Around the lake. It's about three kilometres. We'll **pass by** the old bridge and the small café.  
**Leo:** A café! That's motivation. Maybe after the run we can stop there for breakfast.  
**Mia:** Of course. But first, you have to survive the run. Ready? Go!  
**Leo:** Wow, I'm already out of breath. How do you **keep going** so easily?  
**Mia:** Practice. And I never look at my watch, because then I **lose track of time**, and it feels shorter.  
**Leo:** Smart trick. Honestly, the view is beautiful. I'd never see this if I stayed in bed.  
**Mia:** That's why I love mornings. And remember: **take your time**. We have no race today.  
**Leo:** Okay. I think I can **get through** the first week if you wait for me every morning.  
**Mia:** I promise. And I'll buy you a coffee at the end. Now, last kilometre!
