# Dialog 16: Motivation & Encouragement

**Level:** B1  
**Duration:** 3-4 min  
**Key idioms:** 4

## Characters
- **Coach:** Marcus
- **Athlete:** Nina

## Full Dialogue

**Marcus:** Nina, I see you're wiped out. What's wrong?

**Nina:** I've been training around the clock, but I feel like I'm falling short. Maybe I'm not good enough.

**Marcus:** Listen, it seems to me you have incredible potential. Don't give up!

**Nina:** Really? You think so?

**Marcus:** Absolutely! Break a leg in your next competition. You're all set to win!
