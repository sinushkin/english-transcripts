# Dialog 09: Launching a new product

**Level:** B2 · **Setting:** team meeting: Laura (lead), Sam and Rita (colleagues)  
**Length:** 312 words, about 2.4 min spoken · **Idioms:** 15

## Dialogue

**Laura:** Good morning, everyone. Today we **kick off** the launch of our new app, so let's make this meeting count.  
**Sam:** Morning, Laura. Before we start, I have to say that our beta results **fall short** of what the investors expected.  
**Laura:** I know, Sam. That's why we are here. Rita, did you **put together** the user feedback report?  
**Rita:** Yes, it's ready. The main problem is that users don't **get the point** of our main feature. It confuses them.  
**Laura:** That's a serious issue. We need to **get** the value **across** in the first thirty seconds, or people will leave.  
**Sam:** I **put forward** a simple idea last week: add a short video tutorial on the first screen.  
**Laura:** I remember. I liked it, but I didn't **get back to** you, because the budget question **slipped my mind**. Sorry.  
**Sam:** No problem. Do you think the director will approve? She doesn't always **take** our ideas **seriously**.  
**Laura:** She will, if we show numbers. Rita, can you **take over** the data part and prepare a short presentation?  
**Rita:** Sure. I'll **put in** a few extra hours tonight, and send you a draft by tomorrow noon.  
**Laura:** Perfect. Sam, could you **shoot** the design team **a message** about the tutorial and ask how fast they can make it?  
**Sam:** Of course. I'll write to them right after the meeting. They usually answer within an hour.  
**Rita:** One more thing: I think we shouldn't **put off** the marketing plan. If we wait, we'll miss the spring season.  
**Laura:** Agreed. Let's **take a risk** and move the launch two weeks earlier than planned.  
**Sam:** That's bold. Can we **get by** with our current team, or do we need extra people?  
**Laura:** We'll **get by**, but just barely. If it gets too heavy, I'll ask the director for a freelancer.  
**Rita:** Sounds good. I'm excited. This launch could really change our company.  
**Laura:** Let's stay focused. Meeting again on Thursday, same time. Thanks, everyone.
