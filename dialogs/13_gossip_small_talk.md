# Dialog 13: Gossip & Small Talk at Office

**Level:** B1  
**Duration:** 3-4 min  
**Key idioms:** 4

## Characters
- **Coworker 1:** Amy
- **Coworker 2:** Ben

## Full Dialogue

**Amy:** Ben! Have you heard about the new project? It sounds huge!

**Ben:** Yeah! It seems to me we'll be working around the clock for months.

**Amy:** I know! But at least we're all set with a good team. I really want to hang out more and build team chemistry.

**Ben:** Same! I don't want to fall short on this opportunity. It could be our big break!

**Amy:** Break a leg with that attitude!
