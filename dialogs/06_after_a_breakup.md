# Dialog 06: After a breakup

**Level:** B1-B2 · **Setting:** Max visits his friend Julia, who recently broke up with her boyfriend  
**Length:** 319 words, about 2.5 min spoken · **Idioms:** 10

## Dialogue

**Max:** Julia, I haven't heard from you for two weeks. Are you okay? I was worried.  
**Julia:** Not really. I know it sounds dramatic, but I can't **get over** him. We were together for four years.  
**Max:** It takes time. Nobody can **get over** something like that in a few days. **Take your time**.  
**Julia:** I know. The worst thing is that I keep trying to **hold back** my tears at work, and it doesn't always work.  
**Max:** You don't have to **hold back** with me. Cry, shout, say whatever you want. I'm listening.  
**Julia:** Thank you. To be honest, I've started to **keep to myself** more and more. I turn down every invitation.  
**Max:** I noticed. I don't want to **beat around the bush**, so let me say it directly: it isn't healthy to stay alone all the time.  
**Julia:** I know. But when I try to **socialize**, everybody asks about him, and I want to disappear.  
**Max:** Then meet people who don't know him. Why don't you **take up** something new? A pottery class, maybe, or climbing.  
**Julia:** Climbing? I'm afraid of heights. But maybe it's a good way to **overcome my fears**, all of them at once.  
**Max:** Exactly! I'll go with you. I did a beginner course last year, and the instructors were great.  
**Julia:** Okay, you convinced me. Let's try it on Saturday. If I hate it, I'll tell you immediately.  
**Max:** Deal. And if you want, we can have dinner afterwards. I know a quiet place near the gym.  
**Julia:** That sounds nice. Sometimes I **lose track of time** at home, just sitting and staring at the wall.  
**Max:** Then you need a plan for your evenings. Even a small plan helps you **get through the day**.  
**Julia:** You're right. Yesterday I managed to **get through the day** without checking his messages once.  
**Max:** That's real progress! I'm proud of you. One day at a time, okay?  
**Julia:** One day at a time. Thanks, Max. I'm really lucky to have you.
