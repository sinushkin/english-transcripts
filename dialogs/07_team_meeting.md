# Dialog 7: Team Meeting - Project Update

**Level:** B2  
**Duration:** 3-4 min  
**Key idioms:** 4

## Characters
- **Boss:** Jennifer
- **Team Member:** Mark

## Full Dialogue

**Jennifer:** Alright team, let's discuss the project status. Mark, where do we stand?

**Mark:** We've been working around the clock to meet the deadline. The design phase is complete.

**Jennifer:** Great! It seems to me we're on track. Did we fall short anywhere?

**Mark:** Not really. We took on some extra work, but quality never suffered. The team is definitely wiped out though.

**Jennifer:** I appreciate that. Tell them they can take tomorrow afternoon off. You all did an amazing job. Break a leg with the next phase!

---
