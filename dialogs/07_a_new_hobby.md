# Dialog 07: A new hobby

**Level:** B1 · **Setting:** Pete and Kate talk about Pete's new interest in painting  
**Length:** 321 words, about 2.5 min spoken · **Idioms:** 9

## Dialogue

**Kate:** Pete, you look different today. Happy, relaxed. What's your secret?  
**Pete:** I've decided to **take up** painting. I bought a set of paints last month, and it's wonderful.  
**Kate:** Really? I didn't know you were interested in art. Did you **take** a class, or are you teaching yourself?  
**Pete:** A bit of both. I watch videos, and on Saturdays I go to a small studio. Honestly, I **lose track of time** there.  
**Kate:** That's a good sign. When you forget about time, it means you really enjoy something.  
**Pete:** Exactly. I used to **put off** things like this. I always said "maybe later, when I have more time".  
**Kate:** Me too! I wanted to learn the guitar for ten years, and every year I **put** it **off**.  
**Pete:** Then start now! The older I **get**, the more I understand that "later" never comes.  
**Kate:** You're right, we all **get older** fast. But I'm afraid I'll be bad at it and feel stupid.  
**Pete:** Everyone is bad at the beginning. I painted a tree that looked like a broccoli. But I didn't stop.  
**Kate:** Ha! I'd love to see that broccoli. Do you show your paintings to anyone?  
**Pete:** Not yet. I'm afraid people won't **take** my hobby **seriously**. They'll say "nice" and move on.  
**Kate:** I would take it seriously! Next time, send me a photo, and I'll give you honest feedback.  
**Pete:** Thanks. Actually, the studio is planning an exhibition in spring, and the teacher wants to **put together** a group show.  
**Kate:** That's fantastic! Maybe you should **take a risk** and put one of your paintings in it.  
**Pete:** I'm thinking about it. At the studio, I started to **mingle with people**, and it's easier than I expected.  
**Kate:** See? A new hobby can change more than your free time. It can change your life.  
**Pete:** I agree. Come with me on Saturday, and try it. The first lesson is free.  
**Kate:** Okay, I'll come. But if I paint a broccoli too, you're not allowed to laugh!
