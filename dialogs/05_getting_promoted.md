# Dialog 05: Getting promoted

**Level:** B2 · **Setting:** Ivan tells his wife Olga about a possible promotion  
**Length:** 302 words, about 2.3 min spoken · **Idioms:** 12

## Dialogue

**Olga:** You're home late again, Ivan. How was work? You seem both tired and excited.  
**Ivan:** Both. My boss told me she would **put** my name **forward** for the team leader position next month.  
**Olga:** That's wonderful! So you might actually **get promoted** this year. You've waited for it so long.  
**Ivan:** I hope so. I've **put in** four years of hard work, and sometimes I felt that nobody noticed.  
**Olga:** I noticed. Remember the project that nearly **fell short** of the deadline? You saved it.  
**Ivan:** Yes, but that was a team effort. The truth is, I'm not a **workaholic** like my boss, and that worries me.  
**Olga:** Why would it matter? You do your job well, and you spend time with us **outside of work**. That's valuable.  
**Ivan:** In our company, people respect those who stay late. **Apparently**, a leader must always be available.  
**Olga:** That's nonsense. A good leader needs to **get** ideas **across** clearly, and you do that better than anyone.  
**Ivan:** Thanks. Do you think the director will **take** me **seriously** as a leader? I'm quite young for this role.  
**Olga:** Age doesn't matter. Your team follows you because you never **put** anyone **down** in front of others.  
**Ivan:** That's true. My old boss used to humiliate people, and I promised myself never to be like him.  
**Olga:** See? That's exactly why you deserve this. When will you hear the final decision?  
**Ivan:** In two weeks. If it's positive, we'll **kick off** the new project in April, and I'll **take over** the whole department.  
**Olga:** Then we should celebrate in advance. Let's go to that Italian restaurant on Saturday night.  
**Ivan:** Good idea, but let's wait for the result first. I don't want to jinx it.  
**Olga:** Fine, but I'm sure everything will be fine. You're the right person for this job.  
**Ivan:** Thank you, Olga. Your support means more to me than any promotion.
