# Dialog 01: First week at a new job

**Level:** B1-B2 · **Setting:** office kitchen, Sofia (new) and Mark (colleague)  
**Length:** 346 words, about 2.7 min spoken · **Idioms:** 12

## Dialogue

**Mark:** So, Sofia, how's your first week at the company going? Are you settling in?  
**Sofia:** It's busy! My manager gave us two days to **put together** a presentation, so I've been **putting in** extra hours every evening.  
**Mark:** Well, **take your time**. Nobody expects perfection in the first week, and the deadline is more flexible than it sounds.  
**Sofia:** That's a relief. The real problem is that I tend to **keep to myself**, and I barely know anyone here yet.  
**Mark:** I used to be the same. When I joined, it was really hard for me to **mingle with people** at lunch.  
**Sofia:** So how did you change that? I would love to be less shy.  
**Mark:** The team helped me **overcome my fears**. They just kept inviting me until I said yes. Come to lunch with us tomorrow.  
**Sofia:** Okay, I will. My other problem is meetings. I can never **get my ideas across** because everyone talks so fast.  
**Mark:** Then **take a risk** and speak first. The first person to talk usually sets the direction of the whole discussion.  
**Sofia:** But what if nobody **takes** me **seriously**? I'm new, and I'm younger than most of the team.  
**Mark:** They will. Your idea for the client report was smart, and everyone noticed it. Honestly, you worry too much.  
**Sofia:** Thanks, that means a lot. By the way, is our manager a **workaholic**? She sent me an email at midnight.  
**Mark:** Total **workaholic**. She answers emails **outside of work** hours too, and sometimes she expects the same from us.  
**Sofia:** **Apparently**, she hasn't had a proper holiday for two years. That sounds exhausting.  
**Mark:** It is, but don't copy her. Go home on time and keep your evenings free for yourself.  
**Sofia:** I'll try. Honestly, I'm starting to enjoy this place more than I expected when I signed the contract.  
**Mark:** Good! Let's grab a coffee and go through your presentation together before the weekend. I can give you some feedback.  
**Sofia:** That would be amazing. And thanks for being so friendly. It really helps when you don't know anybody.  
**Mark:** Anytime. We were all new once, and somebody was kind to me, too.
