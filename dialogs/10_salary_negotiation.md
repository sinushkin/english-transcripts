# Dialog 10: Salary Negotiation

**Level:** B2  
**Duration:** 3-4 min  
**Key idioms:** 4

## Characters
- **Employee:** David
- **HR Director:** Patricia

## Full Dialogue

**Patricia:** David, we're pleased with your performance. What's your expectation for the raise?

**David:** Well, I've been working around the clock, and I haven't fallen short on any project. I think a 15% raise is reasonable.

**Patricia:** It seems to me that's quite high. Let me talk to management. Are you all set to discuss this next week?

**David:** Yes, I'm ready. I want to stay here, but I also deserve recognition for my hard work.

**Patricia:** Fair point. Break a leg with your negotiations. We'll find a solution that works.
