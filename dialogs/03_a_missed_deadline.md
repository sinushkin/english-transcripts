# Dialog 03: A missed deadline

**Level:** B2 · **Setting:** manager Karen talks to Tom after a late report  
**Length:** 348 words, about 2.7 min spoken · **Idioms:** 11

## Dialogue

**Karen:** Tom, do you have a minute? I wanted to talk about the quarterly report. It's three days late.  
**Tom:** I know, and I'm sorry. Honestly, our results **fall short** of what I promised, and I didn't want to send it half-finished.  
**Karen:** I understand, but you could have **shot** me **a message** earlier. Silence is the worst way to handle a problem.  
**Tom:** You're right. I meant to write on Monday, but it completely **slipped my mind** because of the client call.  
**Karen:** That call was important, but we have to communicate. When you are late, tell me and I'll **get back to** the client myself.  
**Tom:** Thank you. I've been **putting in** extra hours, but I **put off** the final numbers because I wasn't sure about them.  
**Karen:** What exactly were you unsure about? Maybe I can help, or maybe someone else can **take over** that section.  
**Tom:** The sales data from the south region looks strange. If I'm right, the sales **fall short** of our forecast by twenty percent.  
**Karen:** That's a big difference. I need to know this immediately, not in a late report. This changes our plan for the next quarter.  
**Tom:** Understood. I'll send you the exact figures tonight, and tomorrow I can **kick off** a deeper analysis with the team.  
**Karen:** Good. Let's **get through** this week first. Tomorrow morning, at nine, we'll hold a short meeting and decide who does what.  
**Tom:** I'll prepare a one-page summary for the meeting. I really want to fix this and **take** responsibility **seriously**.  
**Karen:** I appreciate that attitude. Everyone makes mistakes, but not everyone admits them. That's why I still trust you.  
**Tom:** Thank you, Karen. It means a lot, especially after a stressful week like this one.  
**Karen:** One more thing: you don't have to be a hero. If you're drowning in work, tell me early, and we'll find a solution.  
**Tom:** Okay. I'll remember that. Next time I'll write to you on the first day, not on the last one.  
**Karen:** Perfect. Now go home early today. You look like you haven't slept for two days.  
**Tom:** Ha, true. I'll send the figures and then I'm off. Thanks again.
