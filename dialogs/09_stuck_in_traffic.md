# Dialog 9: Stuck in Traffic

**Level:** A2  
**Duration:** 3-4 min  
**Key idioms:** 3

## Characters
- **James:** Driver
- **Jerry:** Passenger

## Full Dialogue

**James:** Ugh, we're stuck in traffic again! This is becoming a routine.

**Jerry:** I know! It seems to me this happens every day.

**James:** Seriously. I've been sitting here for an hour. I'm getting wiped out.

**Jerry:** Why don't we listen to music or a podcast?

**James:** Good idea! At least we're all set with snacks.

**Jerry:** And coffee! This traffic situation won't stop us.
