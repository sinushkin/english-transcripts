# Dialog 19: Peer Tutoring Session

**Level:** B1  
**Duration:** 3-4 min  
**Key idioms:** 4

## Characters
- **Tutor:** Lucas
- **Student:** Anna

## Full Dialogue

**Lucas:** Hi Anna! Ready to study?

**Anna:** Yes! I've been studying around the clock for this exam. I'm wiped out!

**Lucas:** It seems to me you need a break. Let's study together strategically.

**Anna:** Thanks! I don't want to fall short on this test. Can you help me with vocabulary?

**Lucas:** Of course! Let's hang out and go through everything. You're all set!
