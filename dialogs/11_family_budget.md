# Dialog 11: Family budget

**Level:** B1-B2 · **Setting:** a couple, Anna and Victor, discuss money on a Sunday evening  
**Length:** 316 words, about 2.4 min spoken · **Idioms:** 12

## Dialogue

**Anna:** Victor, we need to talk about money. This month we spent much more than we planned, and I don't understand why.  
**Victor:** I think we **fall short** every month because we don't track small expenses. Coffee, taxi, delivery.  
**Anna:** You're right. I want to **put aside** at least twenty percent of our income. Right now, we save almost nothing.  
**Victor:** That's a lot. Can we **get by** on the rest? The rent alone takes half of my salary.  
**Anna:** If we're careful, yes. My friend Dina lives on ten dollars a day for food, and she's fine.  
**Victor:** Ten dollars a day? I bet we can't **get by** on that. We both love to eat out.  
**Anna:** We don't need to be so extreme. But we could cook at home more often, and **put off** buying the new TV.  
**Victor:** Ouch. I've been waiting for that TV for a year. But fine, I can **put** it **off** until autumn.  
**Anna:** Thank you. Also, I read that the boss might **get** you **promoted** soon. Is that true?  
**Victor:** Maybe. I've **put in** a lot of overtime lately, and apparently they noticed. We'll see in March.  
**Anna:** If it happens, don't spend the extra money immediately. Let's **put** it **aside** for our holiday.  
**Victor:** Agreed. Although I think I'm becoming a **workaholic** just to pay all our bills.  
**Anna:** Don't say that. I want you to rest **outside of work**, too. Money isn't everything.  
**Victor:** I know. But a bit of security makes life calmer. Maybe we should **take a risk** and invest a small amount.  
**Anna:** I don't know. I'm afraid of losing everything. I'd prefer to **take our time** and learn about it first.  
**Victor:** Fair. Let's read about it together this month, and decide in spring. No hurry.  
**Anna:** Okay. And let's agree to write down every expense in an app. I'll **shoot** you **a message** whenever I spend something big.  
**Victor:** Good plan. And I'll do the same. We're a team.
