# Dialog 3: Workplace Conflict Resolution

**Level:** B2  
**Duration:** 3-4 min  
**Key idioms:** 4

## Characters
- **Manager:** Sarah
- **Employee:** Tom

## Full Dialogue

**Sarah:** Tom, I need to talk to you. Your recent work hasn't met expectations.

**Tom:** I understand. To be honest, I've been wiped out lately. The workload is overwhelming.

**Sarah:** Look, I get it. But you can't let things fall short. We need to take this seriously.

**Tom:** You're right. What if I work around the clock this week to catch up?

**Sarah:** That's not the solution. Let's sit down and figure this out together. It seems to me we need better communication.

**Tom:** I appreciate that. I'm ready to turn things around. When can we discuss this?

**Sarah:** How about tomorrow morning? I'm all set to help you succeed.
