# Dialog 12: Moving House

**Level:** B1  
**Duration:** 3-4 min  
**Key idioms:** 4

## Characters
- **Friend 1:** Lisa
- **Friend 2:** Tom

## Full Dialogue

**Lisa:** Tom! Thanks so much for helping me move today. I'm completely wiped out!

**Tom:** No problem! Happy to help. It seems to me you needed backup with all these boxes.

**Lisa:** I've been packing around the clock! I don't want to fall short on anything.

**Tom:** You've got this! Want to hang out for a bit and order pizza?

**Lisa:** Absolutely! I'm all set to relax now!
