# Dialog 5: At the Airport

**Level:** B1  
**Duration:** 3-4 min  
**Key idioms:** 4

## Characters
- **Agent:** Robert
- **Traveler:** Susan

## Full Dialogue

**Robert:** Good morning! Do you have your documents ready?

**Susan:** Yes, I'm all set. This trip has been planned for months!

**Robert:** Great! It seems to me your luggage is a bit heavy. Is this your carry-on?

**Susan:** Yes. I've been preparing around the clock. I don't want to fall short on anything.

**Robert:** Let me help you. Your gate is 12. You're good to go!

**Susan:** Thank you! I'm so excited. This is the trip of a lifetime!

**Robert:** Have a great flight! Break a leg with your adventure!
