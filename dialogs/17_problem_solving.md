# Dialog 17: Problem Solving Between Friends

**Level:** B2  
**Duration:** 3-4 min  
**Key idioms:** 4

## Characters
- **Friend 1:** Sophia
- **Friend 2:** Jake

## Full Dialogue

**Sophia:** Jake, I have a big problem. My boss wants me to work around the clock on a new project.

**Jake:** That's stressful. It seems to me you need to set boundaries.

**Sophia:** You're right. But I also don't want to fall short on the project. What should I do?

**Jake:** Let's hang out this week and brainstorm. I'm all set to help!

**Sophia:** Thanks! Break a leg helping me work through this!
