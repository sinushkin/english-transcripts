# Dialog 18: Hotel Check-in

**Level:** A2  
**Duration:** 3-4 min  
**Key idioms:** 3

## Characters
- **Receptionist:** Emma
- **Guest:** Michael

## Full Dialogue

**Emma:** Welcome! Do you have your documents ready?

**Michael:** Yes, I'm all set! I've been traveling around the clock and I'm completely wiped out.

**Emma:** You must be tired! Let me help you with check-in. Your room is ready.

**Michael:** It seems to me this is a great hotel! The location is perfect.

**Emma:** Thank you! Enjoy your stay!
