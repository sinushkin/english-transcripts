# Dialog 1: Job Interview

**Level:** B2  
**Duration:** 3-4 min  
**Key idioms:** 4  
**Target:** Professional vocabulary and career motivation

## Characters
- **Interviewer:** David (HR Manager)
- **Candidate:** Emma

## Full Dialogue

**David:** Emma, tell me about your previous role. How would you describe your work style?

**Emma:** Well, I work around the clock when there are important deadlines. I really take challenges seriously and never fall short on quality. My team was always happy because I'd hang out with them and boost morale.

**David:** That's impressive! What drives you in your career?

**Emma:** To be honest, I was wiped out at my last job, but it made me realize I need to pursue something I'm truly passionate about. I want to break a leg in this new role!

**David:** Great! When can you start?

**Emma:** I'm all set to begin next Monday!

## Learning Points

### Expression 1: "around the clock"

**Q: How do you describe working non-stop?**  
**Hint (RU):** Круглые сутки, постоянно  
**Correct:** I work around the clock when deadlines are tight.
