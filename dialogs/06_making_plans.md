# Dialog 6: Making Plans with Friends

**Level:** B1  
**Duration:** 3-4 min  
**Key idioms:** 4

## Characters
- **Chris:** Your friend
- **You:** Meeting friend

## Full Dialogue

**Chris:** Hey! I haven't seen you in ages. You look wiped out!

**You:** Yeah, work has been crazy. But I'm finally free this weekend!

**Chris:** Perfect! Want to hang out? We could grab coffee or go to that new restaurant.

**You:** Sounds great! I've been craving good food around the clock lately.

**Chris:** Me too! How about Saturday afternoon?

**You:** I'm all set for that. Should we invite others?

**Chris:** Sure! Breaking social anxiety with friends is the best. Let's get everyone together!

**You:** This will be amazing!
