# Dialog 13: Moving abroad

**Level:** B2 · **Setting:** Julia and Paul prepare to move to Canada, talking with Julia's mother  
**Length:** 312 words, about 2.4 min spoken · **Idioms:** 15

## Dialogue

**Mother:** Julia, I still can't believe you're moving to Canada. You've always been a **rolling stone**, but this is far.  
**Julia:** I know, Mum. But Paul and I decided to **take a risk**. Our jobs here don't give us any future.  
**Paul:** We'll **set off** in September. First we'll rent a small flat, and then we'll look for something better.  
**Mother:** And your jobs? Do you have anything arranged? I worry that you'll struggle to **get by**.  
**Julia:** Paul has an offer already. And I'll **take** a few months **off** to learn the language properly.  
**Paul:** My new company even helped us **put together** all the visa documents. That was a big relief.  
**Mother:** It's good they take care of you. But what about the language? You'll need to **get** your ideas **across**.  
**Julia:** That's my biggest fear. But I'm trying to **overcome my fears** step by step. I already chat with a tutor twice a week.  
**Mother:** That's wise. Will you be able to **get around** the city without a car?  
**Paul:** Yes, public transport is great there. The neighbourhood we chose has a metro station five minutes away.  
**Mother:** Then I'll come and **see** you **off** at the airport. And I'll **put aside** some money to visit you at Christmas.  
**Julia:** Mum, don't cry now! You'll **hold back** your tears until the airport, right?  
**Mother:** I'll try, but it's hard. You're my only daughter. Don't **put up with** anything bad, and call me every Sunday.  
**Julia:** I promise. We'll **keep** in touch every week, and we'll send photos every day if you want.  
**Paul:** And remember, a year isn't long. We might even come back, if we don't like it.  
**Mother:** Please don't **put off** visiting us in the summer, either. I want to meet your new friends.  
**Julia:** We'll invite you as soon as we **get over** the first chaos of moving. Maybe in spring.  
**Mother:** Good. Now eat something. You're too thin for a travelling family!
