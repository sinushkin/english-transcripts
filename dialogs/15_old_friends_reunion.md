# Dialog 15: Old friends reunion

**Level:** B1-B2 · **Setting:** Greg and Helen meet after fifteen years in a quiet café  
**Length:** 335 words, about 2.6 min spoken · **Idioms:** 13

## Dialogue

**Helen:** Greg! I can't believe it's you. Fifteen years! I almost **passed by** your table without noticing.  
**Greg:** Helen! You haven't changed at all. Well, I have. I'm starting to **get older**, and my hair shows it.  
**Helen:** Stop it, you look great. So, what happened to you? Last time I heard, you were travelling.  
**Greg:** Yes, I was a real **rolling stone**. I spent six years in different countries and never stayed anywhere long.  
**Helen:** That sounds amazing. I was always the opposite. I **kept to myself** and stayed in my hometown.  
**Greg:** Don't say that. You were never boring. Remember how we used to **lose track of time** at the library?  
**Helen:** Of course! We sat there until it closed, and the librarian had to **see** us **off**. Good times.  
**Greg:** Listen, I don't want to **beat around the bush**. I always wanted to tell you something about that time.  
**Helen:** Oh? Now I'm curious. Go ahead, and don't **hold back**.  
**Greg:** I felt guilty for years. After graduation, I promised to call you, and I never did. It **slipped my mind**, and then it became awkward.  
**Helen:** Greg, it's fine. I was angry for a while, but I managed to **get over** it a long time ago.  
**Greg:** Thank you. It really **got on my nerves** that I never apologised. I'm glad I can finally say it.  
**Helen:** Apology accepted. Honestly, life pushed both of us in different directions. Nobody was to blame.  
**Greg:** True. And what about now? Do you still **take up** new things like before? You loved to try everything.  
**Helen:** Less than before, but I started pottery last year. Sometimes I forget about everything and **lose track of time** again.  
**Greg:** Some things never change. Maybe I should visit your studio. I'm staying in town for a few days.  
**Helen:** Please do. And **take your time** here, there's no rush. Let's order something to eat first.  
**Greg:** Good idea. Coffee and a large piece of cake. My treat, since I owe you one for fifteen years.  
**Helen:** Deal. And next time, don't wait fifteen years to call!
