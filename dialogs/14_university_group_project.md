# Dialog 14: University group project

**Level:** B1-B2 · **Setting:** four students prepare a presentation; Emma coordinates, Jack and Zoe report  
**Length:** 302 words, about 2.3 min spoken · **Idioms:** 16

## Dialogue

**Emma:** Okay, team, we have only five days left. Let's **kick off** the final stage of our project today.  
**Jack:** Sorry I'm late. I forgot about the meeting. It completely **slipped my mind**.  
**Emma:** It's fine, but please, **shoot** us **a message** next time if you can't come. We need to know.  
**Zoe:** I've almost finished my part. I had to **put in** a few late nights, but the slides look good.  
**Emma:** Great! Jack, did you **put together** the data table? Because without it, we can't make conclusions.  
**Jack:** Not yet. I **put** it **off** because the professor hasn't answered my question. I'll **get back to** him today.  
**Emma:** Please do, because our results might **fall short** of the professor's expectations without that table.  
**Zoe:** I think the real problem is that we don't **get the point** of the last chapter. Even the professor said it's tricky.  
**Emma:** Then let me **take over** that chapter. I'll read it again and explain the main idea to everyone.  
**Jack:** Thanks, Emma. Honestly, you always **take** this project more **seriously** than any of us.  
**Emma:** I just don't want to **fall short** at the last moment. A good mark matters for my scholarship.  
**Zoe:** Can I **put forward** an idea? Let's practise the presentation twice before the real one, once alone and once together.  
**Emma:** Good idea. Also, who is going to **get** our message **across** during the speech? We need one main speaker.  
**Jack:** I'd say Zoe. She's the best at explaining complicated things in simple words.  
**Zoe:** Really? I usually **keep to myself** in class, and I'm nervous when people look at me.  
**Emma:** That's exactly why you should do it. It's a good chance to **overcome your fears**. We'll support you.  
**Zoe:** Okay, I'll **take a risk**. But please don't laugh if my voice shakes.  
**Jack:** We won't. And afterwards, we'll **hang out** and celebrate. Pizza is on me!
