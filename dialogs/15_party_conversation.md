# Dialog 15: Party Conversation

**Level:** B1  
**Duration:** 3-4 min  
**Key idioms:** 3

## Characters
- **Person A:** Grace
- **Person B:** Kevin

## Full Dialogue

**Grace:** Hey Kevin! It's been ages!

**Kevin:** Grace! I know! I've been working around the clock at my new job. I'm completely wiped out!

**Grace:** That sounds tough! Want to hang out and relax tonight?

**Kevin:** Absolutely! I'm all set to just chill and enjoy the party!

**Grace:** Perfect! Let's grab some food and chat!
