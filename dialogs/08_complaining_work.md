# Dialog 8: Complaining About Work

**Level:** B1  
**Duration:** 3-4 min  
**Key idioms:** 4

## Characters
- **Friend 1:** Rachel
- **Friend 2:** Mike

## Full Dialogue

**Rachel:** I'm so frustrated! My boss expects me to work around the clock.

**Mike:** That sounds terrible. It seems to me you need a break.

**Rachel:** I know! I'm completely wiped out. No one appreciates what I do.

**Mike:** Have you talked to your boss about this?

**Rachel:** Not yet. But I'm ready to. I'm not going to let them push me around anymore. I deserve better!

**Mike:** That's the spirit! Break a leg with that conversation!
